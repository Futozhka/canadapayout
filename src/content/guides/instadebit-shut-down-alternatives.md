InstaDebit has shut down. The last day to deposit with it was February 27, 2026, and you could withdraw your InstaDebit balance until April 30, 2026. Any money left in an account goes automatically to the linked bank account after 28 days of inactivity. InstaDebit itself points users to Interac instead. For casino payouts, the main replacements are Interac e-Transfer and a bank transfer to your own account; some casinos also offer e-wallets such as MuchBetter.

## What happened to my InstaDebit balance?

According to InstaDebit, a balance left in your account is sent to the bank account you linked to InstaDebit once the account has been inactive for 28 days.

1. **Check the linked bank account.** Look for an incoming payment from InstaDebit after your last activity.
2. **Check whether you still have an old bank account linked.** If you closed or changed the account, the transfer may have gone to, or bounced from, the old one.
3. **Contact InstaDebit if the money is missing.** Use the contact options on its website and keep a record of your messages.

Money still sitting in your **casino** account is a separate matter. It stays with the casino, and you can withdraw it with any other method the casino offers.

**Interac e-Transfer is the closest replacement for most players.** It pays into your own bank account, so there is no extra wallet to manage, and it is the option InstaDebit itself names. If a large payout fails, check your bank's e-Transfer limit first: each bank sets its own.

**A bank transfer is slower but simple.** It is the fallback when Interac is not offered for withdrawals.

**An e-wallet adds a step.** The money lands in the wallet first and you move it to your bank yourself, so check the wallet's own fees before you rely on it.

### Is iDebit the same thing?

No. iDebit is a separate brand with a similar name, and some casinos list both. We could not confirm iDebit's current terms from its own website, so we do not recommend it here. If your casino offers it, read the cashier details before using it for a withdrawal.

## How to switch your casino payouts to a new method

1. **Add and verify the new account first.** In Ontario, a casino may only pay out after the appropriate checks, and only to an account of which you are a legal holder. A partner's or friend's account will not work.
2. **Read the casino's withdrawal rules.** Ontario casinos must tell you about every rule and restriction on deposits and withdrawals, and about every fee before you withdraw. Check which methods you can withdraw to.
3. **Make a small test withdrawal.** Once one payout arrives, you know the method works for your account.
